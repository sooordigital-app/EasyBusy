Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 14, 2026

EasyBusy ("the App") is a cashbook and small business accounting app. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App handles and how.

1. Data We Collect

EasyBusy stores your business, cashbook, and transaction data locally on your device. We do not require account creation and do not collect personal information such as your name, email, or phone number to use the App.

2. Data Storage

All data you enter (business details, cashbook entries, transactions) is stored on your own device. We do not upload, sell, or share this data with third parties.

3. Permissions

The App may request storage-related permissions solely to save your data locally on your device for offline use.

4. Third-Party Services

EasyBusy does not integrate third-party advertising or analytics services that track your personal activity.

5. Children's Privacy

EasyBusy is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from children.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page.
